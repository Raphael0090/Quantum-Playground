GHZ & W states — the Mermin inequality

Three parties each measure X or Y on their share of a three-qubit state. The Mermin witness M = ⟨XYY⟩ + ⟨YXY⟩ + ⟨YYX⟩ − ⟨XXX⟩ is bounded by 2 for any local-hidden-variable model, but a genuinely entangled GHZ state can saturate the algebraic maximum |M| = 4 — a bigger gap than CHSH's 2√2. The W state, although entangled, gives exactly 0 on this particular witness.
